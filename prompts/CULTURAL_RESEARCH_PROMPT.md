# CULTURAL RESEARCH PROMPT

Build a high-coverage Indonesian cultural knowledge base.

## Target coverage

Search comprehensively across official public systems and credible supplemental sources.

Include:

- suku
- bahasa
- adat
- ritus
- tradisi
- WBTb
- indigenous communities
- customary territories
- traditional knowledge
- land/water/resource management
- food
- clothing
- crafts
- architecture
- music
- dance
- theatre/performance
- oral traditions
- games/sports
- cultural institutions

## Requirements

For each record:

- source
- geography
- community/language relationship
- context
- values/principles
- sensitivity
- suitability for gameplay
- adaptation note

Do not automatically gamify sacred/restricted practices.

Deliver a coverage report showing which provinces/regions/categories have rich coverage and where gaps remain.
