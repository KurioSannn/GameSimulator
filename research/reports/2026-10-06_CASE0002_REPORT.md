## Game adaptation (game/data/cases/CASE-0002*.json)
- Trigger: month ≥ 20, presented at month 21 (Oct, year 2), after CASE-0001.
- Shock: damage ∝ BNPB loss by location (DERIVED), anchored at Palu = 45 (design): Palu 45, Sigi 37.4, Donggala 14.6, Parigi Moutong 3.5.
- Choices (SIMULATED):
  - a) rebuild in place: fastest, trust ↑; residual risk recorded.
  - b) relocate from the red zone: most expensive and slowest; trust dips, then recovers with permanent housing at month 12, which also brings services.
  - c) self-build stimulus: cheapest; welfare ↑ for 3 months; uneven building quality.
- Follow-ups (simulated consequences):
  - B "Pulang ke Zona Merah" opens only on the relocation path and only while Palu/Sigi trust stays low. It is inspired by the documented return of residents, but labeled simulated.
  - C "Mutu Rumah Swadaya Dipertanyakan" opens after a year on the stimulus path.
- **Not simulated (stated in the case and decision D-043):** earthquake recurrence. Relocation's long-term risk reduction is recorded as memory (`palu2018_red_zone_cleared`, `palu2018_red_zone_residual_risk`) for the legacy assessment. No seismic hazard model is invented before calibration data exist.

End-of-term outcomes in Palu (golden master, seed 11):
- relocation: damage 4.2, services 53, fiscal 41
- rebuild: damage 5.0, trust 47.2, fiscal 43
- stimulus: damage 5.2, services 51.5, fiscal 44

No option dominates.
