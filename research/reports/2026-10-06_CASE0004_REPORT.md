6. **Core causal graph:**
   - governance choice → `fire_prevention`: enforcement (all 6 peat provinces, immediate), restoration (Kalteng, after 6 months, × services), or community governance (Kalteng, after 3 months, × trust)
   - → next fire season (CASE-0004-R, impact × (100 − prevention)/100)
   - → damage, environment, welfare → logistics → food → price → sentiment
   - ban → livelihood loss (Kalteng welfare) → sentiment; ban/community → trust
   - costs → fiscal_space and policy commitments → sector budgets (crowding out)
7. **Decisions:**
   - a) total burning ban with enforcement;
   - b) peat restoration (enacts POL-KEBENCANAAN-RESTORASI-GAMBUT, BRG-inspired);
   - c) community fire governance (enacts POL-KEBENCANAAN-API-ADAT, from CULT-0001).
8. **Immediate effects:** fiscal −4 (a); suppression −8 (a) or −4 (b, c) damage in Kalteng after 1 month; Kalteng trust −2 (a); recognition trust + (c, implementation phase).
9. **Delayed effects:** restoration prevention +2/month from month 6; community prevention +1/month × trust from month 3; livelihood loss −0.4/month for 12 months (a).
10. **Regional effects:** the ban protects all six peat provinces at once; restoration and community governance protect Kalteng only. Test: only the ban lowers Riau's fire-season impact.
11. **National effects:** fiscal_space; policy commitments crowd out sector budgets; food-price spillover from damage.
12. **Sentiment effects:** through damage, welfare and price everywhere, and trust in Kalteng. Term-end national sentiment 48.5–49.0 across paths (golden master).
13. **Local wisdom research:** CULT-0001, Dayak Ngaju *handel* group land preparation in Desa Mantangai, Kapuas. Documented elements: supervised burning by deliberation (*barunding*) with *damang* and *mantir*, firebreaks 3–6 m, customary fine *jipen* if fire spreads, and high dependence on fire. Pergub Kalteng 27/2019 later codified limited controlled burning, combining technique and local wisdom (bans in the dry season, on peat over 50 cm, and during declared emergencies). CULT-0002 records *tabat* (water blocks), informational for now. Registry: research/culture/CULTURE_REGISTRY.md.
14. **Cultural mechanic design:** POL-KEBENCANAAN-API-ADAT.
    - Fire prevention is scaled by trust (proxy for community legitimacy, range 0.3–1.3×); there is no livelihood cost; uncertainty ±30%; slower than a ban.
    - **Ritual excluded.** The mechanic is labeled FICTIONAL abstraction; the record stays REAL description. It is not generalized to all Dayak communities or all of Kalteng.
    - Validator: `cultural_basis` must resolve to a researched record. Human cultural review is pending (G-22).

**Explainable trade-off (golden master, seed 11):**
- The **ban** ends with the best fire outcomes and national sentiment, but the lowest Kalteng trust and a livelihood follow-up.
- **Restoration**'s payoff is mostly beyond the term; it is still building at the first fire season.
- **Community governance** underperforms here because post-fire trust (~46) is below the reference (55), which is exactly the documented dependence on collective compliance.
