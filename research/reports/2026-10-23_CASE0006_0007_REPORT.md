## CASE-0006: Kemarau Panjang di Sabu Raijua (presented in month 10, October of year one)

### Sources
| Source | Supports |
|---|---|
| BMKG press release, 8 Sep 2023 | El Niño +1.504 and IOD +1.527; the 2023/24 rainy season arrives later than usual, peaking Jan–Feb 2024 |
| BMKG Staklim NTT, 20 Sep 2023 | 19 of 28 NTT season zones start late (December), incl. Sumba, Rote Ndao and most of Timor. The release also cites "16 ZOM (57%)", a different comparison; noted. |
| ANTARA quoting BPBD NTT, 26 Aug 2023 | Provincial *siaga darurat* until October 2023; six regencies declared status |
| BPS NTT table (source BNPB), 22 Feb 2024 | Drought, "terdampak dan mengungsi": Sabu Raijua 31,020, Kab. Kupang 1,044, NTT 32,064 persons. No deaths or injuries; no other regency has a value. Parsed from the JSON embedded in the page. |
| BPS NTT rice 2023 | Context only (Kupang 83,408 t; Sabu Raijua 6,645 t); no losses inferred |

### Geography
Direct effects apply only to 53.20 and 53.01. The other emergency-status regencies (Belu, Rote Ndao, Alor, Sumba Barat Daya) are context only.

### *Mamar*
Kept out of the mechanics (CULT-0003, informational only):
- Ndun et al. 2021 measures predicted erosion (lowest under *mamar* at Teunbaun, Amarasi Barat).
- Ngaji et al. 2021 reports declining sustainability.
- Neither supports a drought or water mechanism.
- The spring link is only cited second-hand (source not retrievable).
- The scale is villages, not the whole regency.

### Choices
The case event sets Sabu Raijua welfare −3 and services −2, and Kab. Kupang −1 and −0.5.

| Choice | Effects | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| a, emergency clean water | services +1.5/month for 3 months, trust +1, fiscal −2 | fastest relief; leaves nothing for the next dry season |
| b, food and livelihood support | welfare +1/month for 4 months (more where welfare is low), trust +0.5, fiscal −3 | protects welfare; adds no water |
| c, water resilience (embung, boreholes, piping) | fiscal −5, trust −1 now; infrastructure +10 and services +2 after 9 months | no visible relief now; the only lasting change |

**Follow-up CASE-0006-R:** the next dry season (from month 22, Aug–Oct). Its hit is scaled by water infrastructure (`scale_by` gap).

## CASE-0007: Puskesmas Tanpa Dokter di Pegunungan (presented in month 16)

### Sources
| Source | Supports |
|---|---|
| Kemenkes *Profil Kesehatan Indonesia 2023* (foreword June 2024; SISDMK) | Puskesmas without a doctor in 2023: Papua Pegunungan 54.3%, Papua Tengah 43.5%, Papua Barat Daya 26.9% |
| same | Lowest puskesmas-per-kecamatan ratios in Papua Tengah, Papua Barat, Papua Barat Daya and Papua Pegunungan; accreditation far below target. The appendix tables are glyph-encoded, so only narrative statements are used. |
| Kemenkes *Profil* with 2022 data (pre-split Papua) | 7.6% of puskesmas met the 9-workforce standard vs 56.1% nationally; 61.4% had a doctor vs 96.0%. Research record only: no publication month is available. |
| Kemenkes, 14 Feb 2023 | Nusantara Sehat special assignments (2 years, remote priority; 7,250 active in 2,540 puskesmas) |
| Unverified, not used | Otsus 20% health share; 2024–2025 secondary percentages |

### Geography
Direct effects apply to Papua Pegunungan and Papua Tengah (16 regencies), uniformly within each province, because the finest reliable resolution is the province. Papua Barat Daya is context only.

### Opening adjustment
Services −0.2 × the documented gap (−10.9 / −8.7), labeled "Penyesuaian data kasus", not an event.

### Choices
| Choice | Effects | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| a, strengthen regional health offices (both provinces) | fiscal −2; after 6 months, services +0.25/month for 30 months (larger where services are weakest) and trust +1 | cheapest, builds capacity, slowest |
| b, rotating central health teams (both provinces) | fiscal −4, plus −2 after 12 months; services +0.5/month for 24 months from month 2 | fastest broad gain, costly, ends with the deployment |
| c, mobile services and referral (Papua Pegunungan only) | fiscal −5; services +1/month for 6 months; trust +1; infrastructure +6 after 12 months | fastest in the worst province; Papua Tengah untouched |

**Follow-ups:**
- **CASE-0007-A:** after a, if Papua Pegunungan services are still below 45 at month 25.
- **CASE-0007-B:** after b, when the deployment ends (month 43).

| Papua Tengah services at Q+6 | 42.8 | 47.3 | 39.3 (unchanged) |
| Papua Pegunungan infrastructure | 50 | 50 | 56 at Q+5 |
| Fiscal space at Q+1 | 44 | 42 | 41 |
