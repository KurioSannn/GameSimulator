# Execution report: CASE-0003 — 2022 cooking-oil crisis and CPO export ban

Date: 2026-10-06 · Agent: Claude Code · Human gates unchanged (no BPS key; no BNPB manual files).

1. **Case and why:** the first non-disaster case (food/industry/economy). It adds Sumatra (new island group) and a national price channel, exercises the budget (subsidy vs. lost export revenue), and has a documented regional conflict of interest (consumers vs. palm-oil farmers).
2. **Sources:**
   - DPR RI Puslit "Isu Sepekan" III, May 2022 (official, archived with checksum)
   - ANTARA, 20 May 2022 (Coordinating Minister Airlangga Hartarto)
   - ANTARA, 6 Apr 2022 (President, cabinet session)
   - Kontan, 19 Mar 2022 (Permendag 11/2022)
   - Excluded: an unverified shortage headline; post-ban Kalimantan Barat prices.
3. **Location:** national consumer effect in all 514 kab/kota; producer effects in Riau (14), Jambi (15), Sumatera Selatan (16), all 40 kab/kota (evidence is province-level).
4. **Domain:** food price, edible-oil supply, export policy, smallholder income, social assistance.
5. **Core cross-system chain:** export/supply policy → `price_pressure` (new, D-045) → price target in every region → price level → welfare → sentiment. In parallel: producer income → welfare and trust in Riau, Jambi and Sumsel. Subsidy and lost export revenue → fiscal_space → sector budget room.
6. **Choices:**
   - a) temporary export ban: fast relief; producers hit, Riau about 2× Sumsel per the documented TBS declines; export revenue lost.
   - b) DMO + special price: no fiscal cost, slower, compliance risk.
   - c) bulk-oil subsidy + targeted cash: most expensive, producers safe, prices stay higher; cash scales up where welfare is low.
7. **Branching:**
   - a → A "Petani Sawit Menuntut Ekspor Dibuka" (only if Riau welfare < 54);
   - b → B "Kepatuhan Pasok Dalam Negeri Lemah" (only if price pressure > 0.03 after month 44);
   - c → C "Bantuan Habis, Harga Masih Tinggi" (only if pressure > 0.04 after month 42).
   - All three are labeled simulated consequences, and each is exclusive to its path (tested).
8. **Regional effects:** producer welfare and trust (a, and lightly b); welfare-gap-scaled cash (c).
9. **National effects:** price pressure on all regions; fiscal_space.
10. **Sentiment effects:** via price and welfare in every region; via trust in producer provinces. Golden master, seed 11: national sentiment at term end 47.3–49.0 across choices.
11. **Cultural connection:** none. No documented local practice is specific to this case, so none was added.
