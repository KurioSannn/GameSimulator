# 24 DEMO AND JUDGING STRATEGY

## Purpose

The competition build should communicate the strongest aspects within a short judging session.

## Demo storyline

Recommended sequence:

1. Start game as Nara Adinata.
2. Show real Indonesia map.
3. Select a province/regency.
4. Show real-data-driven regional profile.
5. Receive a compelling real-world-inspired case.
6. Read briefing and data.
7. Consult an advisor.
8. Make a decision.
9. Advance a simulation tick/turn.
10. Show a regional consequence.
11. Show a national consequence.
12. Show sentiment changes by region.
13. Trigger or preview the next consequence.
14. Show local-wisdom governance option where appropriate.
15. Show legacy summary.

## What to emphasize

### Ide and creativity

Real Indonesia + data-driven simulation + local wisdom as mechanics + branching causal cases.

### Gameplay/UX

Decision-making under trade-offs and visible consequences.

### Stability/performance

Full-regional simulation with a controlled optimized architecture.

### Visual/audio

Coherent, readable 2D vector style and purposeful audio.

### Impact

Civic/data literacy, cultural awareness, systems thinking.

## Do not demo everything

A short, polished, coherent slice is better than an unstable tour of every feature.
