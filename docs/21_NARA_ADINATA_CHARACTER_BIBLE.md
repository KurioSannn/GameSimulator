# 21 NARA ADINATA CHARACTER BIBLE

## Identity

**Name:** Nara Adinata  
**Role:** fictional President of Indonesia  
**Status:** player character

## Locked traits

- analytical
- calm
- empathetic
- pragmatic
- curious
- occasionally over-analytical

## Core philosophy

- evidence before assumption
- development should reach regions
- modernization should not erase identity

## Character conflict

Nara begins with confidence that evidence helps reveal answers. Through the story, he learns that evidence clarifies trade-offs but rarely creates perfect answers.

## Dialogue tone

- natural Bahasa Indonesia
- concise but intelligent
- not bureaucratic in every line
- calm during crisis
- occasional situational humor through advisor interactions
- never a caricature of a real politician

## Voice direction

Conceptual direction:

- male
- approximately 40s
- calm
- warm
- moderately low pitch
- authoritative but approachable

Exact voice model/tool is provisional until the team validates licensing and quality.

## Visual direction

- Indonesian male, approximately late 40s
- professional
- modern formal clothing
- subtle batik usage where contextually appropriate
- not designed to resemble a specific living political figure

## Expression set

- neutral
- thinking
- concerned
- confident
- surprised
- frustrated
- relieved
- disappointed
- serious
- happy

## Character arc

```text
DATA → DECISION → CONSEQUENCE → REFLECTION → ADAPTATION
```
